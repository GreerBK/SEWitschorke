# How to update your website

Your website's text and photos live in this project, and you can edit all of it
yourself through a friendly editing site called **Pages CMS**. No coding — it
looks like filling out a form. Every change you save goes live on the website
automatically within a few minutes.

## One-time setup (about 10 minutes)

1. **Create a free GitHub account** at [github.com](https://github.com) if you
   don't have one. GitHub is where the website's files are stored.
2. **Accept the invitation.** Your site admin will send a collaborator
   invitation to your GitHub account — accept it (it arrives by email and also
   appears at github.com/notifications).
3. **Sign in to the editor** at [app.pagescms.org](https://app.pagescms.org)
   using the **"Sign in with GitHub"** button. Choose the `SEWitschorke`
   project.

That's it. From now on you only ever need step 3.

## Editing text

After signing in you'll see sections in the left sidebar:

- **Home Page** — the quote at the top of the home page.
- **About — Bio** — your biography, one box per paragraph. You can add,
  remove, and reorder paragraphs.
- **About — Awards** — the awards list (year, award title, winning work).
- **Books** — each book's title, cover, description, year, reading level, and
  order link. Turn on **"Show on home page"** for books you want featured.

Edit what you like, then click **Save**. The website rebuilds itself and your
change is live in about 2–3 minutes. Refresh the page to see it.

## Adding state photos to the Gallery

The gallery shows one photo per state. To add one:

1. In Pages CMS, open **Media** in the sidebar and go into the
   `gallery` folder.
2. Upload your photo. **The file name tells the website which state it is**,
   so before uploading, rename the photo on your computer to the state's name,
   ending in `.jpg`. Examples:
   - `texas.jpg`
   - `north carolina.jpg`
   - `New York.jpg`

   Capital letters and spaces are fine — the uploader tidies them up
   automatically (`New York.jpg` becomes `new-york.jpg`). Just make sure the
   name is the state and nothing else (no `IMG_1234.jpg`).

Two important notes about photos:

- **The photo must be a real JPEG (.jpg).** iPhone photos are often saved in
  Apple's HEIC format, which won't display for most visitors even if you
  rename it to `.jpg`. When sending a photo from your iPhone, use
  Settings → Camera → Formats → **"Most Compatible"**, or share/export it as
  JPEG first.
- Square-ish photos look best — the gallery crops each photo to a square tile.

States without a photo yet simply show a gray tile with the state's name, so
there's no harm in filling the map in gradually.

## If something looks wrong

You can't permanently break anything. Every change is saved in the site's
history, so your site admin can restore any earlier version. If a change
doesn't appear after ~5 minutes or something looks off, just tell your admin
what you edited and when.
